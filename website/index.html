About

Built by someone who's been
on both sides of the spec sheet.

FlatToFactory was built out of a simple frustration: most fashion design resources stop at the sketch. But the sketch is just the beginning. Getting a garment made — really made, factory-ready, at the right cost and quality — requires a whole other set of documents and vocabulary that most designers learn only by doing it wrong first.

I'm a working fashion professional and professor at a university fashion program. I've spent years watching talented students and small brand founders lose money, lose time, and lose their first production run because nobody taught them what a tech pack actually needs to contain.

FlatToFactory is the resource I wish had existed — practical, professional, and built around how factories actually work. Whether you're a student submitting your first class assignment, a small brand getting ready for your first sample, or a freelance designer building your practice, these tools are designed to make you look like you've been doing this for years.

Every template in this shop is built to real industry standard. Every document is factory-tested. And everything you need to ask a manufacturer the right questions without second-guessing yourself is in here.
